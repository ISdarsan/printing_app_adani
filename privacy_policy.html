Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 8, 2026

Application: FoodPrint ("the App")

Developer/Publisher: Vizmart ("we", "our", or "us")

This Privacy Policy outlines how FoodPrint collects, uses, and safeguards information when authorized staff and canteen personnel use the mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • User Authentication: Email address and authentication credentials provided by your organization to identify authorized cashiers and canteen managers.
  • Transaction & Customer Credit Records: Customer names and phone numbers optionally entered during credit billing transactions to maintain accurate credit balances and send transaction notices.
  • Expense Documentation: Photos taken using your device's camera or uploaded from your photo gallery as receipts/proof of canteen procurement and operational expenses.

2. Device Permissions Used

  • Camera & Media / Storage: Used strictly to capture and upload photo proofs of expense receipts. Photos are not used for any biometric or advertising purposes.
  • Bluetooth (BLUETOOTH_CONNECT & BLUETOOTH_SCAN): Used solely to discover, pair with, and print receipts to thermal ESC/POS Bluetooth printers. Location derived from Bluetooth scanning is not gathered, tracked, or stored (permission flag neverForLocation is enforced).
  • Internet: Used to synchronize billing transactions, expense records, and credit ledger data securely with Firebase cloud infrastructure.

3. How We Use Information

All data collected is used solely to provide core canteen point-of-sale functionality, including:

  • Printing customer receipts via thermal printers.
  • Generating daily sales reports and monthly cash reconciliations.
  • Maintaining customer credit ledgers and audit records.
  • Managing verified operational expense reimbursement records.

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or share your data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

The App utilizes the following trusted infrastructure services:

  • Google Firebase (Firestore & Firebase Authentication): For secure database management, user verification, and cloud synchronization in compliance with Google Cloud security standards.

5. Data Retention and Account Deletion

Transaction records and expense receipts are maintained for business accounting and audit purposes as mandated by the organization. Users or canteen staff may request the removal or deletion of their account or personal credit records by contacting our administrative team at the email below.

6. Security

We value your trust in providing operational data and implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical security measures to safeguard data stored within the app and Firebase services.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this document periodically for any updates.
